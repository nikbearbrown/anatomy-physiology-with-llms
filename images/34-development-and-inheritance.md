## Visual Strategy

**Primary learning path:**
1. Start with sperm-egg fusion (Concept 1) to hook attention
2. Move to cleavage and implantation (Concept 2) to show rapid early growth
3. Introduce germ layers and their fates (Concept 2) as the "body plan revealed"
4. Show placenta (Concept 2) to illustrate maternal-fetal relationship
5. Present fetal circulatory shunts (Concept 3) to show adaptation to fetal life
6. Close with inheritance Punnett squares (Concept 3) to make probability concrete

**Visual hierarchy in text:**
- Sperm-egg fusion: high detail (mechanism-critical)
- Germ layers: high detail (foundational to all organ systems)
- Placenta: moderate detail (selective exchange is the key point)
- Fetal circulation: high detail (shunts are unintuitive; visual essential)
- Punnett squares: moderate detail (worked examples may work with text alone, but visuals reinforce ratio logic)
